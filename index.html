About

What is Buddhir Dokan?

Buddhir Dokan is a small initiative in Agartala built around a simple idea: sometimes, talking to someone with a different life experience can help us see our own situation differently.

We offer a quiet space where you can talk freely, be heard without judgement and exchange perspectives with people who have experienced different stages of life.

We won't promise to have all the answers. We'll listen, share our experiences and offer suggestions where we can. Our hope is that you leave feeling a little lighter and with greater clarity about your situation.

WhereIn-person, Agartala. Exact location shared after confirmation
How longAbout 60 minutes
CostCompletely free during pilot
LanguageBengali, English, Hindi - whichever you are comfortable in

Who will you be talking to?

Our team brings together people from different walks of life, including retired government officers, a homemaker, an engineer-entrepreneur and a healthcare professional.


Together, we bring around 250 years of combined life experience—different perspectives, personal stories, difficult decisions and lessons learned along the way.

We are not a counselling clinic, and we do not present ourselves as qualified therapists or professional counsellors. We are people willing to listen, share what life has taught us and help you look at your situation from another perspective.


We may not always have the answer you're looking for. But we'll give you our time and attention, and treat your concerns with respect.

How it works

What happens during a session?

1

Start with a conversation

We'll introduce ourselves, settle in with chai and some snacks, and help you feel comfortable.

2

Talk at your own pace

Share as much or as little as you're comfortable with. You don't need to follow a script or explain everything perfectly.

3

Exchange perspectives

We'll listen and, where appropriate, share experiences, opinions and suggestions that may help you look at things differently.

4

Leave with your own decisions

Our aim is to help you think more clearly, not to hand you a list of things you must do.

5

Tell us how we did

We'll invite your honest feedback so we can make this initiative more useful to others.

Every person and situation is different. We cannot promise a solution to every problem, but we can promise to give the conversation the time and attention it deserves.

Trust & privacy

A few things you should know

No judgement

You can speak openly without expecting a lecture or being told how to live your life.

Your decisions remain yours

We'll offer suggestions based on our understanding and experiences. You are free to agree, disagree or decide that a suggestion isn't right for you.

Your conversation is private

We don't record, write notes about or maintain a file of what you discuss. We collect your contact details only to arrange and confirm your appointment; they are not published publicly. Please share only what you feel comfortable sharing.

Completely free during our pilot

No charge, no hidden payment and no sales agenda. No pressure to book again.

What Buddhir Dokan is—and isn't

Buddhir Dokan is

A space for thoughtful conversation, listening and general life guidance.

It is NOT

A therapy service, a mental-health clinic, or a place for psychological diagnosis or treatment. We do not offer legal advice or guidance on criminal matters.

If you need clinical psychological support or help with an urgent mental-health concern, please contact a qualified professional or appropriate emergency service.

Need urgent support? Please call Tele-MANAS toll-free: 14416 or 1-800-891-4416. Available 24/7.

We believe in being honest about what we can offer and recognising when someone needs a different kind of help.

What kind of problems can I discuss?

Stress, loneliness, family or personal concerns, difficult decisions, feeling directionless or anything else that's on your mind. You can also visit simply because you'd like someone sensible to talk to.

Will you tell me what I should do?

We'll share our perspective and suggestions based on what you've told us and what we've experienced in life. We won't impose a decision. Your choices remain your own.

Who will I meet during the session?

You'll meet members of our team from government service, homemaking, engineering and healthcare. We confirm arrangements before your visit.

Will my conversation remain private?

We don't record or document details. Contact details are used only to arrange the session and are not published publicly.

Is the session really free?

Yes. During our pilot, completely free. No hidden charges, no obligation to return.

How long does a session last, and where does it happen?

Usually about an hour, in-person in Agartala. Location is shared when confirming the appointment.

How do I book a session?

Fill the appointment request form with contact details and preferred time. You don't need to describe your personal problem in the form.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Please inform us at least 24 hours before.

What if I don't find the conversation useful?

That's completely fine. No obligation to return. We welcome honest feedback - this is an experiment.
